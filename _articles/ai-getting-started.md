# From chatbot to doing stuff

If you use AI like a fancy search engine, you're getting a fraction of what it can do. The jump is to stop asking it questions and start making it do things. This is the fastest method I know, and it takes the least thinking.

## Step 1: Get a paid account (5 minutes)

Pick one vendor and pay the US$20 to 30 a month. If you haven't picked, use ChatGPT. Install the phone app, the desktop app, or both, whichever you'll actually open.

If the monthly price is a dealbreaker, this probably isn't for you yet.

## Step 2: Connect everything (1 hour, day one)

Put an hour in your calendar for today or tomorrow, and don't skip it. Open the connectors settings (some vendors call them plugins or apps) and connect every tool you use. Don't be selective.

- Email (Gmail, Outlook)
- Calendar
- Files (Drive, Dropbox)
- Notes (Notion, Notes)
- Tasks (Todoist)
- Anything else you open weekly

Then test each one with a real request:

- What's on my calendar tomorrow?
- Find the latest email from my accountant.
- What's the most recent file I edited in Drive?

If a test fails, disconnect that app and connect it again.

Each connection gives the AI access to that account. Read the permissions screen before you approve it.

## Step 3: Stop opening the other apps (2 weeks)

For the next two weeks, do your work through the AI app instead.

- Write emails there.
- Read your inbox there. Ask for a summary of what's unread.
- Check your calendar there.
- Find files there.

When you do open the original app, write down what you were trying to do. That is the next thing to ask the AI to handle.

## Step 4: Assume it can do everything (from then on)

When you want to do something and don't know how, ask the AI before you search Google. Assume it can be done and see how far you get. You will hit limits, and they arrive later than you expect.

Do the hour on day one. Stick with the two weeks. After that you won't want to go back.
